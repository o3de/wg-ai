# O3DE AI Working Group - Meetings

## Meetings

WG-AI meets **weekly on Mondays at 10:00 Pacific / 19:00 CET** in the [WG-AI Discord channel](https://discord.com/channels/805939474655346758/1052633353695793172). The time was agreed on [o3de/tsc#159](https://github.com/o3de/tsc/issues/159). When US or EU daylight saving time changes, the Pacific time is fixed and the CET time may shift by an hour.

Future meetings are held based on the [agendas on GitHub](https://github.com/o3de/wg-ai/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Amtg-agenda).

Please [reach out on Discord](https://discord.gg/Mc6jStmuMK) or [sign up on the mailing lists](https://lists.o3de.org/groups) to get notifications for the recurring events.

## Who can attend

Anyone. *You do not need anyone's permission to get involved and contribute to the project.* WG-AI is open to every member of the community, whether or not you use AI tools. Concerns about AI matter here as much as enthusiasm for it.

## Agenda items

The agenda for each meeting is an [issue in this repository](https://github.com/o3de/wg-ai/issues?q=label%3Amtg-agenda) labeled `mtg-agenda`, created from the **Meeting Agenda** issue template. Propose a topic by commenting on the next agenda issue.

## Notes

The note taker commits notes to [notes/](notes/readme.md) as `YYYY-MM-DD-WG-AI-meeting.md` and links them from the agenda issue.
